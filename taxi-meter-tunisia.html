Privacy Policy

Taxi Meter Tunisia • Developed by Pure Tunisia

Taxi Meter Tunisia is a free application developed by Pure Tunisia. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Taxi Meter Tunisia application.

Information Collection and Use

1. Personal and Device Information

Taxi Meter Tunisia does not require users to create an account and does not collect or store personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.

2. Location Data

The current version of Taxi Meter Tunisia does not request or access your device's location or GPS data.

3. Camera and Images

The current version of Taxi Meter Tunisia does not request or access your device's camera and does not collect, upload, or store photographs or images from your device.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Taxi Meter Tunisia does not collect, store, or share personal user data with third parties.

Fare calculations and related calculations performed by the application are processed locally on the device.

The application may require Internet access for services necessary for the operation of the application, but Internet access by itself does not mean that personal user data is collected.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when changes to the application affect how user information is handled. Any updated version will be published at this location.
